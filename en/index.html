Privacy

FREE + PRO · ANDROID
01

Game data on your device

Save files, gameplay statistics and settings are processed in Android app storage to preserve your progress and preferences. The planned features do not require a player account. These game data are not intended to be sent to AppsMakerDeluxe.

02

Backups and Pro import

You choose a destination when exporting a backup through Android’s file picker. Hiberlume Pro can import a file you select. The export is saved at your chosen destination and may contain your personal progress. Keep it safely and delete it from that location when no longer needed.

03

Internet, ads and analytics

The game is planned as an ad-free offline game without advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Playing is not intended to require an account or an ongoing internet connection. Your app store processes data under its own terms, such as when you download or purchase an app.

04

Permissions and background activity

The planned gameplay features do not need access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone. You choose whether to import or export a file. The Android app is not intended to play in the background or send saves to cloud storage. Its manifests and backup settings will be checked before release.

05

Deleting data and retention

As planned, game saves, statistics and settings remain in app storage until you clear the app data or uninstall it. A save you exported remains at the destination you selected, where you can delete it. Information in email enquiries is used to reply. Please do not email passwords or private save files.

06

Website and contact

This static GitHub Pages site loads no analytics or advertising scripts, sets no first-party tracking cookies and loads no external fonts. GitHub processes technical connection and site-use data under its own privacy statement. Your email provider transmits messages you send. Privacy enquiries: AppsMakerDeluxe Studios, appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com.

GitHub privacy statement

Development status

The game is still in development. This notice describes planned data handling. Before release, we will compare it against the permissions, SDKs and storage features in the finished Free and Pro apps and update it if needed.
